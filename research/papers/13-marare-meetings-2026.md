# MARARE — From business meetings to requirement artifacts

- **Cite:** *From Business Meetings to Requirement Artifacts: An Agentic AI Approach with MARARE*. DOI [10.1145/3786167.3788405](https://doi.org/10.1145/3786167.3788405)
- **Read:** abstract only
- **Role:** real-time meeting agent (one talks, others extract/verify)

## Results (tiny sample)

5 meetings, 5–8 minutes each, LLM-as-judge:

| Metric | Mean ± SD |
|--------|-----------|
| Coverage | **80.0 ± 11.2%** |
| Semantic similarity | **0.86 ± 0.05** |
| Hallucination rate | **14.3 ± 6.2%** |

Model choice affects coverage vs hallucination.

## Use

Cite as concurrent 2026 agentic capture. Not a competitor on critique. Hallucination ~14% is a **warning band** for our spec writer — we should measure invented requirements similarly.
